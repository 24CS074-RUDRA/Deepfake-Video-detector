QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What defines a synthetic deepfake video? ▼
A deepfake is a video or audio media asset generated using deep learning neural networks (such as GANs or diffusion models) to fabricate or manipulate facial identity, facial expressions, or vocal speech patterns.
How does DeepShield AI detect facial manipulation? ▼
DeepShield AI combines spatial pixel boundary analysis with 468 3D facial landmark mesh tracking. It evaluates micro-jitter between frames, corneal light reflection consistency, blink rate regularity, and acoustic lip-sync alignment.
What video formats and resolutions are supported? ▼
We support standard video container formats including MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, and WEBM. Uploads support file sizes up to 500MB and resolutions up to 4K UHD.
How are forensic reports generated? ▼
Upon scan completion, a complete report is compiled with SHA-256 style report hashes, confidence score breakdowns, detected anomaly lists, and frame-by-frame timelines. Reports can be exported as printable legal PDFs or raw JSON logs.
